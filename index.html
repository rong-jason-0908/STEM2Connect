Tremor Detector

Draw a spiral with your mouse. the model will read your motion using the x,y, and timestamp, and measure the tremor for your result

Mouse only. Trackpads and touchscreens are not supported: in our small test, stop-and-go cursor motion on trackpads scored as tremor-like (false alarms).
This is a tool not a diagnosis. it was trained on tablet-pen spirals from 61 Parkinson's patients and 15 controls, and calibrated on 16 healthy mouse users. It hasn't been validated on real patients using a mouse. Caffeine, nerves, stress, a bad mouse, etc. can also cause tremor-like signals.
Start in the middle and spiral outward, 2–3 turns, at a comfortable pace. Hold the button down the whole time.

How the score is built

1) your cursor position is recorded every few milliseconds
2) smooth out the slow, intended motion. this way, we remove the spiral and isolate the tremor
3) split the wobble into frequencies. parkinson's sits at around 4-6 Hz
4) your score is compared to healthy users, weighted by a simple regression model and pushes are summed
